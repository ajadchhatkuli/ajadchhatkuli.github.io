We analyze the theoretical framework of denoising diffusion policy optimization and show that updating only the latter timesteps of the diffusion RL can be harmful for diversity, contrary to earlier conclusions. We then propose an incremental Feynman-Kac training scheme that achieves the best-yet alignment-diversity trade-offs.
